Full8/COPR11339909 subsequently passed in 1965 seconds, including the 1142-second
kernel compile and 831 module signatures. Six signed RPMs were verified and

Both boots report `controller_state=ready`, `crm_mode=firmware-managed`,
`probe_phase=ready` and error zero. The controller returns to runtime suspend;

native ROM admission, startup synchronization and idle release on these boots.
The front I2C controller and sensor DT nodes remain disabled; no physical
camera frame has been captured.

Two bounded 180-second external recordings contain 10,794 frames and 8.4 MiB
of video. Numerical analysis and transition-frame review found no recurrence
of the previous sustained gray handoff. Daylight reflections made the older
center-region neutral-color heuristic classify the boot logo and wallpaper
incorrectly, so the console region and visual transitions were checked as well.
Brief console clearing and the later GUI transition remain separate behavior.

The first 45-sample touch observation stayed ready throughout. The repeat had
one uncalibrated/not-ready sample at uptime 119.58 seconds and was ready again
at the next sample. Both observations retained zero SPI I/O errors and fault
flags; packet-decode errors remain present. The reason for that transient is
unresolved, and it does not establish full touchscreen stability or explain
the earlier full7 health rejection.
